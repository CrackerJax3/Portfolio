Corbusier Saudi Style Sofa

Project Details

3-meter-wide sofa fabricated from cut and welded 1.5 in rebar with caster wheels, custom Saudi cultural pattern upholstery and foam. Taught 3 female Saudi apprentices to measure, prep, weld, and upholster from zero experience to finished product. Installed at multiple Saudi facilities including MSX Saudi Arabia and Dhahran Ahliyya Schools.

Highlights

  • 3 m wide · 1.5 in rebar frame
  • Custom Saudi cultural pattern upholstery
  • Trained 3 apprentices from scratch
