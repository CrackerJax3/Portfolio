Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 10, 2026  ·  Subotix LLC

This Privacy Policy describes how Subotix LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile applications ("App") available on Google Play. This policy applies to all users of our App, which is intended for users aged 13 and older.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: Gameplay data, scores, progress, session duration, features used, and interactions within the App.
  • Log Data: Crash reports, error logs, timestamps, and diagnostic data automatically generated when you use the App.
  • Advertising Identifiers: If the App displays ads, we or our advertising partners may collect your advertising ID (e.g., Android Advertising ID) to serve relevant ads.
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized data about how users interact with the App, collected through third-party analytics services.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying personal information unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g., via a support request).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App
  • Analyze usage trends and fix bugs
  • Display advertisements (if applicable)
  • Ensure the security and integrity of the App
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

Our App may use third-party services that collect information independently under their own privacy policies. These may include:

  • Google Play Services — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (if ads are shown) — AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase (if analytics are used) — Firebase Privacy Policy

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services used within the App.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors who assist in operating the App (e.g., analytics, crash reporting), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as a business asset.

5. Data Retention

We retain collected data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Anonymized and aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for analytical purposes.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information promptly. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the App, please contact us at the address below.

For users between 13 and 17, we encourage parental involvement and do not use their data for targeted advertising without appropriate consent where required by law.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

  • Opt out of personalized ads: You can reset or opt out of ad tracking in your Android device settings under Google > Ads.
  • Analytics opt-out: You may opt out of analytics collection through your device settings or the App's settings menu where applicable.

9. International Users

If you are accessing the App from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
