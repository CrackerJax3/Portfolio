Project Touareg

Project Details

Full mechanical and electrical restoration of a Volkswagen Touareg — repaired a seized camshaft, replaced all belts, pulleys, and tensioner, cleared oil passageways of sludge, cut and coded new keys, and re-coded the headlights.

Highlights

  • Seized camshaft repair
  • Belts, pulleys & tensioner
  • Oil passageway sludge removal
  • Key cutting & coding
  • Headlight recoding
