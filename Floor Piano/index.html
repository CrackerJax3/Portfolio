UNSDG MakeyMakey Floor Piano

Project Details

Maintained, repaired, and calibrated laser cutters and 3D printers across two summer programs serving 200+ students aged 8–12. Designed and ran two main projects: DIY Slot Cars from recycled materials, and a 4×12 ft LED floor piano programmed in one week. 120 participants contributed UN SDG artwork laser-scanned into the piano keys. The piano was demonstrated to Saudi government officials and Sipchem executives at the program showcase.
