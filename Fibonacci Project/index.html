Fibonacci Project

Project Details

Aluminum tubular sculpture of a Fibonacci Spiral — 30 ft tall, 48 ft wide — completed in 12 days from concept to installation. Designed in CAD, fabricated using a tube roller and TIG welding, and installed on site. Trained students from Denmark and France in CAD design, tube rolling, welding, and measurement throughout the build.

Highlights

  • 30 ft tall · 48 ft wide
  • Concept to installation in 12 days
  • Tube roller · TIG welding
  • Mentored students from Denmark & France
