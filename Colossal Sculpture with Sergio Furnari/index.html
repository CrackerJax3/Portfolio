Colossal Sculpture with Sergio Furnari

Project Details

Procured and operated 3D printing equipment to produce a life-size sculptural bust of Cristiano Ronaldo, commissioned to celebrate CR7's 40th birthday. The sculpture was mounted on a Jeep and debuted at Times Square as part of a national tour.

Highlights

  • Life-size 3D printed bust of Cristiano Ronaldo
  • Commissioned for CR7's 40th birthday
  • Debuted at Times Square, New York City
  • Mounted on Jeep for national tour
